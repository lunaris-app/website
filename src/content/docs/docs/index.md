Lunaris is a desktop reader for manga, manhwa, manhua and light novels, for macOS,
Windows and Linux.

1. [Install Lunaris](/docs/install/) for your system.
2. [Add a source](/docs/sources/): open **Extensions**, and under **Available** install
   the sites you read from the [Lunaris extensions](https://github.com/lunaris-app/extensions) repo.
3. Find a title in **Browse** and add it to your library.

## Around the app

- **Library**: your titles, split into Comics and Light Novels, with
  categories such as Reading, Plan to read and Completed, which also show in the
  sidebar. Sort and filter them, or select several to move to a category or another
  source at once. Lunaris warns you before adding a series that's already there.
- **Updates**: new chapters for titles in your library. Lunaris can check in the
  background and queue new chapters for download.
- **History**: what you read, grouped by day, with your reading time and chapters read.
- **Browse**: search the extensions you've installed.
- **Downloads**: chapters saved for offline reading. The queue carries on after a
  restart, and the page shows how much space each title takes. Turn on **Delete after
  reading** in **Settings > Downloads** to free the space once you've read a chapter.
- **Local files**: point **Read local files from** in **Settings > Downloads** at a folder
  of CBZ or ZIP files or image folders, and read them like any other source.
- **Reader**: chapters open where you left off. Read comics paged, in two-page spreads or
  scrolling, with the view set for all titles or just one, and go full screen. `[` and `]`
  go to the previous and next chapter. The view panel can crop page borders, scroll on its
  own and add a warm tint for night reading. Right-click a page to save or copy it.
  Light novels can be read aloud. Turn on incognito (**Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+N**) to read without
  saving history.
- **Title page**: keep your own notes on a title, and when a source lists chapters from
  several scanlation groups, hide the ones you don't want.
- **Tracking**: sign in to AniList, MyAnimeList or MangaUpdates in **Settings > Tracking**,
  then use **Track** on a title's page. Chapters you finish update your progress there.
- **Settings**: language (English, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean or
  Simplified Chinese), themes, reading mode, page fit, novel typography, keyboard shortcuts,
  and [backup, restore and export](/docs/backup/).

Lunaris doesn't host or include any content. Titles come from extensions you install,
which read from third-party sites.
