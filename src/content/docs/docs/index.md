Lunaris is a desktop reader for manga, manhwa, manhua and light novels, for macOS,
Windows and Linux.

1. [Install Lunaris](/docs/install/) for your system.
2. [Add a source](/docs/sources/): open **Extensions**, add a repo URL under
   **Available**, and install the sites you read.
3. Find a title in **Browse** and add it to your library.

## Around the app

- **Library**: your titles, split into Manga & Comics and Light Novels, with
  categories such as Reading, Plan to read and Completed.
- **Updates**: new chapters for titles in your library. Lunaris can check in the
  background and queue new chapters for download.
- **History**: what you read, grouped by day.
- **Browse**: search the extensions you've installed.
- **Downloads**: chapters saved for offline reading.
- **Settings**: themes, reading mode, page fit, novel typography, keyboard shortcuts,
  and [backup and restore](/docs/backup/).

Lunaris doesn't host or include any content. Titles come from extensions you install,
which read from third-party sites.
