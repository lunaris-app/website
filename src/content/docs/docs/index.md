Lunaris is a desktop reader for manga, manhwa, manhua and light novels, for macOS,
Windows and Linux.

1. [Install Lunaris](/docs/install/) for your system.
2. [Add a source](/docs/sources/): open **Extensions**, and under **Available** install
   the sites you read from the [Lunaris extensions](https://github.com/lunaris-app/extensions) repo.
3. Find a title in **Browse** and add it to your library.

## Around the app

- **Library**: your titles, split into Comics and Light Novels, with
  categories such as Reading, Plan to read and Completed, which also show in the
  sidebar. Sort and filter them, or select several to act on at once.
- **Updates**: new chapters for titles in your library. Lunaris can check in the
  background and queue new chapters for download.
- **History**: what you read, grouped by day.
- **Browse**: search the extensions you've installed.
- **Downloads**: chapters saved for offline reading. The queue carries on after a
  restart, and the page shows how much space each title takes.
- **Reader**: chapters open where you left off. Read comics paged, in two-page spreads or
  scrolling, with the view set for all titles or just one, and go full screen. `[` and `]`
  go to the previous and next chapter.
- **Tracking**: sign in to AniList in **Settings > Tracking**, then use **Track on
  AniList** on a title's page. Chapters you finish update your progress there.
- **Settings**: themes, reading mode, page fit, novel typography, keyboard shortcuts,
  and [backup, restore and export](/docs/backup/).

Lunaris doesn't host or include any content. Titles come from extensions you install,
which read from third-party sites.
